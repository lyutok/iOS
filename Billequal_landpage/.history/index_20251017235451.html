Split bills easily — no math, no stress.

Whether it's dinner with friends or a weekend trip, Billequal helps you calculate tips and share the bill in seconds.
Choose to split equally or customize for each person — and instantly share the results with everyone.

* Requires Apple iOS 12.3+

Features

  • Add a tip with one tap
  • Split equally or by custom amounts
  • Built-in calculator for flexible totals
  • Share results in one message

I built this app because I needed it myself — and maybe you do too.
It's not reinventing the wheel, just my own version, made with care and curiosity.
If it helps even one person the way it's helped me, that's reason enough to share it.
